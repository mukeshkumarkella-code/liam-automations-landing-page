FAQ

Questions small businesses ask about this service

Will an AI receptionist answer every question?

No. It should handle common questions and collect details, then route complex or sensitive issues to a human.

Can this work after hours?

Yes, after-hours inquiry capture and follow-up is one of the strongest use cases.

Is this good for clinics or contractors?

Yes. Clinics, wellness businesses, contractors, and home service companies are high-fit use cases.
