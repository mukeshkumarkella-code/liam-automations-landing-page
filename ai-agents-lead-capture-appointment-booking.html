FAQ

Questions small businesses ask about this service

Is an AI agent different from a chatbot?

Yes. A chatbot mostly answers questions. An AI agent can help move a workflow forward through qualification, follow-up, routing, and booking support.

Can an AI agent replace my team?

The goal is not to replace your team. The goal is to handle repetitive steps and help humans respond faster.

Can it connect to my calendar?

Often yes, depending on your calendar and booking process. This is reviewed during the audit.
