Privacy Policy

Liam AI Automations collects the information you submit through the audit request form or Google Calendar booking page only to respond to your inquiry, manage your appointment, and understand your automation needs.

Information collected

The form or booking page may collect your name, phone number, email address, company name, website, city or service area, business type or industry, automation interest, current process, main problem, preferred timeline, contact preference, appointment time, and related intake answers.

How the information is used

Your information is used to reply to your request, schedule and prepare for a free AI automation audit, send appointment updates, and recommend relevant next steps for your business. It is not sold or used for unrelated purposes.

Form and booking services

The website uses a third-party form endpoint to deliver form submissions by email. Appointment scheduling and video-meeting details are handled through Google Calendar and Google Meet. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Do not submit sensitive passwords, private customer records, payment details, or confidential business data through either path.
